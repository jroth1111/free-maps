Latest performance results

The pinned Ubuntu Lighthouse 13.4.0 acceptance matrix recorded 98–100 Performance medians, a 96 minimum individual score, and all 30 route/profile/cache rows at or above the 98 median floor. Accessibility, Best Practices, SEO, and Agentic Browsing were 100 throughout.

Mobile and iPad: 100 on every route, cold and warm. Desktop: 98 cold and 98–99 warm. Production remains on v0.2.0 until this reviewed candidate is promoted.

Under the hood

  • Map data: © OpenStreetMap contributors
  • Basemap schema/style/assets: Protomaps
  • Tile archive: PMTiles containing vector MVT tiles
  • Browser renderer: Free Maps vector canvas; MapLibre adapter available
  • Hosting: Cloudflare Worker + R2 range reads
  • Demo markers: synthetic GeoJSON data clustered by the vector renderer

No Google Maps components or requests are involved.
