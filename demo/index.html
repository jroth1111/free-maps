Free Maps v0.3.0

A map explorer you can own

Framework-neutral elements, explicit renderer wiring, same-origin protected PMTiles, and deterministic synthetic Melbourne data.

Accepted 19 July 2026

Accepted performance results

The live-candidate Lighthouse 13.4.0 matrix recorded 98–100 Performance medians, a 96 minimum individual score, and perfect Accessibility, Best Practices, SEO, and Agentic Browsing scores in all 90 reports.

Under the hood

  • Map data: © OpenStreetMap contributors
  • Basemap schema/style/assets: Protomaps
  • Tile archive: PMTiles containing vector MVT tiles
  • Browser renderer: Free Maps vector canvas; MapLibre adapter available
  • Hosting: Cloudflare Worker + R2 range reads
  • Demo markers: synthetic GeoJSON data clustered by the vector renderer

No Google Maps components or requests are involved.
